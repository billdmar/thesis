The thesis

We initiate coverage of Deckers Outdoor (DECK) with a Buy rating and a 12-month price target of $128, ~28% above the current $99.68. The market has re-rated DECK down to ~13x earnings and ~9x EV/EBITDA — near its 52-week low — on a single fear: that revenue growth, having decelerated from +24% to +10%, is rolling over. We think that fear is overpriced.
